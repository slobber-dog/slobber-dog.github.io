// about

slobberdog is a creative and technical r&d lab led by meg white. we specialise in: problem finding and problem dissolving, strange machines, proto-things, anti-disciplinary systems, objects, interfaces, experiments.

we work across hardware, software, and the fuzzy places in between. our focus is novel technical work: prototypes, automations, connected devices, strange workflows and systems that need to be invented before they can be implemented.

// workshop materials

frontier ai, agentic workflows, software, electronics, embedded systems, interfaces, data and knowledge systems, reverse engineering, offensive security, experimental design, evaluation, research and whatever else the problem requires.

technologies, disciplines and investigative methods are all materials. we bring the ones the problem requires to the bench.
