slobberdog is a creative and technical r&d lab led by meg white. we specialise in: problem finding and problem dissolving, strange machines, proto-things, anti-disciplinary systems, objects, interfaces, experiments.

in practice, we operate across the physical, digital, and secure domains. bridging electrical engineering (electronics, renewables), offensive security (oscp), and agentic software, we specialize in high-assurance prototyping—building robust automations, auditing complex infrastructure, and designing secure, connected hardware.
