slobberdog is a creative and technical r&d lab led by meg white. we specialise in: problem finding and problem dissolving, strange machines, proto-things, anti-disciplinary systems, objects, interfaces, experiments.

we work across hardware, software, and the fuzzy places between them. our focus is novel technical work: prototypes, automations, connected devices, strange workflows, and systems that need to be invented before they can be implemented.
