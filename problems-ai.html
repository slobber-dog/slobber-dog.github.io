// some opportunity, somewhere

you know ai could be useful, but you don't know where to begin.

finding tasks across your day that ai can participate in is not hard. what is actually difficult is figuring out where frontier ai can create meaningful value inside of real work and what context, judgment, safeguards and evidence the system will need.

touches:

how we approach it

  • learn how the work happens now, including the unofficial version;
  • find recurring units of judgment or action worth investigating;
  • drill into these with quick pilots, small experiments, surveys of existing/prior art;
  • produce artefacts like prototypes and/or action reports and/or architecture roadmaps;
  • evaluate them with the people whose expertise the system must support.

an initial engagement might produce

a map of promising opportunities, a working problem frame, an experimental prototype and evidence about whether the idea deserves further investment.
