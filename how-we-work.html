// how we work

slobberdog is a boutique research and development lab.

we are very, very good at r&d. and we are very, very bad at office theatre.

we work best when there is room to think, test, build, revise and collaborate with you.

we prefer to do this from our own workshop and lab spaces. that is where our best thinking and doing happens. we will be delighted to visit your office when the work calls for it, but most of the time we will be in the lab.

we like useful communication. meetings are critical when they sharpen the work, unblock a decision, relieve an anxiety, clarify a confusion or surface something important. jumping on a call for the sake of having it in the calendar is not a productive part of the process for us.

we prefer frankness. it is important that you feel comfortable telling us what you think, what you want, what you are worried about, and what constraints matter to you. we will do the same. the strongest work comes from real context, honest chats and clear decisions.

we ask for trust in the execution. if the work needs an external designer, developer, fabricator, producer, researcher, specialist or bespoke little toolchain, we will say so. part of the job is knowing who and what is needed to make the thing real.

if this version of working together sounds like relief rather than friction we are probably the right kind of partner for your project.
