ballpark ranges

engagement investment (aud) typical duration

prototype sprint $20k – $35k 2–4 weeks

what affects cost?

The complexity of the workflows, the number of integrations, data preparation requirements, compliance hurdles, and change management all drive where your investment sits. We break projects into small, focused phases so that value is realised quickly and risk remains low.
