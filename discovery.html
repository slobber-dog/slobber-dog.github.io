stage 1 of 5 — what's real saved locally
q1

meditate on a recent experience that was vexing to you. recall what conditions led to it occurring, how you (/your team) responded, and what if anything happened as a result?

pick one specific, real episode. avoid generalisations and hypotheticals.

q2

what would the experience have been instead, if we were in a dream world?

describe the outcome rather than focusing on a product or technology.

q3

whose version of the problem is this?

q4

describe the decisions and actions that take place from the moment of the trigger to the final outcome.

this is a step-by-step answer. focus on each choice, handoff, system interaction, bottleneck and informal workaround.

add as many steps as you need. “it goes through the usual process” is not yet a mechanism.

q5

what do you currently think needs to change or be built?

q6

if you were to make changes, what do you think would become worse, harder, slower, riskier or more expensive?

q7

what cannot be changed?

q8

q9

what's the strongest real-world signal that this assumption is failing?

what's the source of that signal?
q10

do a brain dump of every way you could test this dangerous assumption.
