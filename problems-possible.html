// is this possible?

you have an idea, but don't know whether it's possible.

feasibility is rarely one question. we break the idea into the claims that must be true, identify the dangerous assumptions and create the smallest experiments capable of proving or falsifying the decision.

touches:

how we approach it

  • make the intended effect and operating context precise;
  • separate physical limits, engineering difficulty, integration risk and unknowns;
  • rank assumptions by importance and weakness of evidence;
  • prototype the dangerous parts before polishing the easy ones;
  • define evidence for proceeding, changing direction or stopping.

an initial engagement might produce

a feasibility frame, risk map, experimental programme, proof-of-principle build and a much better answer than yes or no.
